title: Why I'm writing this
---

Let me be honest with you before you read a single chapter: **I am not an
expert.** I'm not an analyst. As I write this, I don't really know anything
about picking stocks. That is the whole point of this book.

Here is how it started. My dad asked me to pick a stock. I did — and I
talked about it like I knew exactly what I was doing. I didn't. I was just
acting confident. My dad saw right through it. He told me:

> "You're careless, and you don't really care — because it's not your money
> at stake."

That stuck with me. So he made me a deal. If I could show him I was serious
about actually learning, I'd put in **all the money I've saved up until
today** — and that's more than it sounds. My dad started slipping small
amounts into my piggy bank the day I was born, and he's kept it up on every
birthday, Diwali, and Christmas since. When my grandparents visit each year
they used to bring gifts; I asked them to put the money in the piggy bank
instead, and once the rest of the family saw that, they started doing the
same. Add my own pocket money and what I've earned doing small jobs for my
parents and relatives, and that piggy bank quietly grew into real savings. My
dad said he'd add some of his own on top. Real money. My money. Now that it's
at stake, I have to care.

So this book is me learning, in public, with real money on the line. I'm
trying to learn two things: how to find a company that is actually *worth*
owning, and — the harder part — how to **wait**. To sit tight long enough.
Maybe I never sell. Maybe I sell too early and it turns out to be a mistake.
Maybe I get the whole thing wrong. I don't know yet. That's exactly what
makes it worth writing down.

I want to learn to think the way **Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, and
Mohnish Pabrai** think. I am nowhere close. This is chapter one of trying.

A few things I want to be crystal clear about, because they matter:

- **I'm 15 as I start this book.** I can't legally have my own brokerage
  account yet, so I invest through a **custodian account with my mom.**
- **The money is mine** — everything I've saved so far. I size each position
  to the opportunity: usually a small starter, then I add as the reasons get
  stronger, not a fixed amount every time.
- **I plan to keep this going for years.** I'm 15, and I've just started doing
  chores around the house that earn me pocket money; as I get older and have
  more time, I want to work part-time and put what I earn into investing. My
  parents also made me a promise: if I ever run out of my own savings but can
  give them a clear, honest reason for a stock I want to buy, they'll put the
  money in so I don't have to stop — the plan is to keep this book going until
  I finish my education and start earning in the real world.
- **Nothing here is advice.** I am not telling you to buy or sell anything.
  I'm a beginner writing down my own journey so I can look back and see what I
  learned. Please don't copy my trades — I might be wrong about every one of
  them.

That's it. Buy right, sit tight, and watch the paint dry with me.

— Krupesh
